## Usage

Omit `CommentThread.onReply` when the viewer cannot comment: existing replies remain visible,
while the reply composer is hidden. Omit edit/delete/reaction callbacks according to the returned
record capabilities. An open edit returns to read mode if edit permission is removed; server
handlers must still enforce current permission at commit.

```tsx
import {
  CommentComposer,
  CommentList,
  type CommentData,
} from "@/components/ui/comments";

<CommentList
  comments={comments}
  count={total}
  hasEarlier={!!cursor}
  onLoadEarlier={loadEarlier}
  highlightedId={hashCommentId}
  onEdit={(id, body) => updateComment(id, body)}
  onDelete={(id) => deleteComment(id)}
  onCopyLink={(id) => copy(`${url}#comment-${id}`)}
  composer={<CommentComposer onSubmit={(body) => postComment(body)} />}
/>;
```

`CommentList` is a record's discussion: an `h2` "Comments" with the count and, when `onOrderChange`
is set, an "Oldest first" / "Newest first" toggle; "Load earlier" when older comments exist; the
comments, with a small centred day label ("Today", "Yesterday", "Sep 25") wherever the day changes
(`dayDividers`); and the composer — at the end oldest first, and right under the heading newest
first, where the newest comment is. Pass `comments` oldest first; `order="newest"` reverses them.
With no comments, the composer sits right under the heading — no empty-state line (pass
`emptyText` for one). A host that lays out its own threads uses `CommentListHeader`,
`CommentDayDivider` (or `useCommentDayDividers`) and `CommentListSkeleton` for the same frame.

The look is Linear's, and the spacing is identical on web and mobile. **A thread is one card** — a
1px `border-border`, `rounded-lg`, the light `bg-muted/30` fill, no padding of its own — whose
comments are rows 12px in from its sides: the first comment, then "Show N more replies" (a
full-width muted row with a chevron, `hiddenReplies` + `onShowReplies`; "Show less" with
`onHideReplies`), then the replies, each over a hairline (`border-border/50`). A row is a header
— a 24px avatar, 8px to the name, the badge, the relative time and "(edited)" — then, 4px under it,
the body. The first comment's body runs the row's full width; **a reply's body is indented to the
name** (32px). At the card's foot, over a hairline, sits **the reply row**: the viewer's avatar
(`viewer`), a one-line "Write a reply…" box with no border or fill of its own, and attach and a
round ↑ Send at its end. It grows with the text, Esc leaves it, and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter sends.

`CommentComposer` — a record's new comment — is a **bordered card**: the text on top (two lines at
rest, about twelve before it scrolls inside, "Add a comment…"), the draft's files under it, and a
toolbar row whose end holds the optional `attachments` slot, a muted paperclip (whenever
`onImageUpload`, `onFileUpload` or `onAttachFiles` is set) and the round ↑ Send — a quiet grey disc
while there is nothing to send. Its border darkens subtly while it holds focus. Cmd/Ctrl+Enter
sends, and Send's tooltip names the shortcut. On a touch screen every round control gets a 40px
target. `replyingTo={{ name }}` shows "Replying to name ×" above the box and the "Write a reply…"
placeholder; × or Esc calls `onCancelReply` and keeps the text. The `files` slot is a draft's
attached files (pending cards with progress and ×), part of what Send posts. With `onAttachFiles`,
files pasted or dropped on the box join that tray — while files are dragged over it, a dashed
"Drop files to attach" overlay shows. With `hasFiles`, Send is enabled with no text;
`CommentThread`'s `composer` takes the same options for its reply row.

Editing a comment turns its body into a compact `Input`-like box and its header actions into a
ghost ✕ Cancel and a primary ✓ Save (disabled while empty or unchanged), both with tooltips naming
Esc and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter. The parts take data and callbacks and keep only transient UI state. A
callback that returns a promise drives the saving or posting state. If the promise rejects, its
message appears under the editor. Bodies are Markdown, edited with `TextEdit` (no toolbar; a
reduced slash menu; no drag handles) and rendered with `MarkdownView`. Times read in the compact
form — "now", "19m ago", "3h ago" — with the exact time on hover.

On its own (a `CommentList`), each comment is its own card, and `renderReplies` nests one level of
replies as rows inside it. A comment's images show as the images themselves — `CommentMedia`, a
wrapping row of rounded previews at most 200px tall, opening the `FileViewer` (wrap a comment's or
a task's files in one `AttachmentPreview` to page through them all); other files are compact
`Attachment` cards. A long name truncates. The card's border never changes on hover or focus. The
hover actions sit at the header's end — add reaction and ⋯, identical ghost `icon-sm` buttons,
shown on hover or focus inside the comment, kept while their popup is open, and always shown on
touch screens.

Each comment is anchored at `#comment-<id>`. Pass that id as `highlightedId` (on `CommentList` or
`CommentThread`) to tint it and scroll it into view. The ⋯ menu is sized to its content: Reply (on
a touch screen, inside a thread, focusing its reply row), Copy link (`onCopyLink`) and Copy text
(`onCopyText`, the Markdown), then — over a separator — Edit and a destructive Delete when the
comment's `canEdit` or `canDelete` allows them. Delete calls `onDelete` at once, with no
confirmation dialog: the host removes the comment and offers Undo (a toast). A soft-deleted
comment keeps its author and time over "This comment was deleted.", with no actions.

Reactions come from each comment's `reactions` and show as [`Reactions`](/docs/components/reactions)
pills under the body, with an add-reaction button after them. With `onReactionToggle`, a pill
click toggles and an "Add reaction" button joins the hover actions too, Slack style.

### Drafts

`defaultValue` restores text the viewer left behind. On `CommentComposer` the editor opens on it
with Send enabled, and after a successful post the box starts empty (`onValueChange("")` fires).
On `CommentThread`, `composer={{ defaultValue }}` opens the reply box on the draft;
`composer.onValueChange` reports every change and `""` once the reply posts, and
`composer.posting` holds the box busy — Send shows its spinner and neither it nor

<kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> sends — while the host finishes an upload
in the box. An edit draft is reported by `onEditValueChange(commentId, value)`
on `CommentItem`, `CommentList` and `CommentThread`: the text on every change,
then `null` when the edit is saved or cancelled. Together they let a host keep
drafts and warn before unsaved text is lost.

To bring an edit back after a remount (a route hidden and shown again), control which comment is
being edited with `editingId` and `onEditingIdChange` on `CommentList` or `CommentThread`, and hand
the saved text back through `editDefaultValue(commentId)`: that comment mounts in editing mode with
its box on the restored text, and Save is enabled while it differs from the body. On a single
`CommentItem` the same text is its `editDefaultValue`. Without `onEditingIdChange` each comment
keeps its own editing state, as before.

### Threads

`CommentThread` is also one discussion about a piece of text, the way Google Docs shows it beside the
page: the quoted words (a button when `onQuoteClick` is set, to find the highlight), the first
comment, its replies, and the reply row
(<kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> sends; the box clears when `onReply` resolves and keeps the
text when it rejects). ✓ in the header calls `onResolve`. Pass `composer` to give the reply box
`mentions`, `mentionHref` and uploads, and `renderAttachments` to show each comment's files.
`active` lifts the card beside its highlight. Lay threads beside the text with
[`CommentMargin`](/docs/components/comment-margin).

#### Resolved, orphaned and collapsed

A resolved thread says who resolved it and when, and offers Reopen (`onReopen`). An `orphaned`
thread — its text was deleted — says "Original text was removed" instead of the quote. `collapsed`
shows the first comment, "N replies" (a button with `onExpand`) and the last reply, with no reply
row.

### Mentions, uploads and attachments

`CommentComposer` passes `mentions`, `mentionHref`, `onImageUpload`, `onFileUpload` and
`onUploadError` to its editor, exactly as on [`TextEdit`](/docs/components/text-edit).
`CommentItem`'s `attachments` renders under the body — an `AttachmentGroup layout="list"` of the
comment's files — and its `mentionHref` links the mention chips in the body. Pass `mentionHref` to
`CommentList` and it reaches every comment in the list; without it the chips are plain labels.

## Accessibility

- The section is labelled by its heading. The comments are a list, and replies are a nested list
  named "Replies".
- Each comment's ⋯ trigger is named for its author ("Actions for comment by Asha Rao").
- The relative time shows the exact time on hover or focus. "(edited)" does the same with the edit
  time.
- "Show N more replies" / "Show less" is a button with `aria-expanded`; a day label is a
  `separator` named for its day.
- Errors are announced with `role="alert"`.
- Reaction pills are toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`) named with the emoji and count.
- A thread is an `article` named for its first comment's author; its ✓ is a button named
  "Resolve", and the reply box is a textbox named "Reply".

| Key                               | Action                            |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Send the comment, or save an edit |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd>                    | Cancel an edit, or leave a reply  |
