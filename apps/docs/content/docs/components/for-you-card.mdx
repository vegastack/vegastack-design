## Usage

```tsx
import { ForYouCard } from "@/components/ui/for-you-card";

<ForYouCard
  verb="approve"
  from={{
    name: "Expense checker",
    avatar: <PersonAvatar person={agent} size="2xs" />,
  }}
  title="Ravi's taxi claim, ₹4,200"
  facts={[{ label: "Over policy by", value: "₹1,200", tone: "warning" }]}
  deadline={{ label: "Due today", urgent: true }}
  ifNobodyAnswers="It goes to Finance on Friday."
  state={card.state}
  options={card.options}
  onOption={(optionId) => answer(card.id, optionId)}
  onSnooze={snooze}
  onSkip={skip}
/>;
```

The card is laid out for the 26rem Inbox panel first and the phone's For you page: one column,
facts and buttons wrap, nothing wider than the panel. Every card says what happens if nobody
answers.

## Examples

### Verbs and bodies

The verb shows as a small label with its icon: Answer, Approve, Review, Fix or Suggest. The body is
the source's own: a `question_form` source renders `AgentQuestionForm`, other `sourceBody` kinds
render through `renderBody`, and `children` holds an inline `AskCard`, a draft or a suggestion's
preview. "Fewer like this" is offered on suggestions only.

### Options

The options are the source's, in its order, with their kinds as the server names them. The
preferred one (`primary`) is drawn secondary: nothing on the card is a filled primary. `decline`,
`skip` and `neutral` are outline; `navigate` is a link; `inspect` opens the evidence and never
settles the card. Skip leaves For you without deciding, so it reads apart from Decline.

### States

`sending` keeps the pressed option's label with the bolt and holds everything else. `submit_failed`
says the answer didn't save and frees the options again. Settled, the card reads as its outcome
("Approved by Priya in Slack · 14:02"); a skip reads "Skipped", not as a decline. `stale` keeps
only its links to the new version. `withdrawn` with `access_lost` shows only "No longer available
to you": nothing of the source and no action.

### In the Inbox

Inside an `Inbox`, the card is a row of its `InboxGroup` or `InboxList`, with the same behaviour as
an `InboxItem` (both use `useInboxRow`). Give it the card's id as `value`: J / K walk to it, the
Inbox's active row selects it (`selected` is then ignored), Enter or a click on the card opens it
through the Inbox's `onOpen` (a click on an option, a field or the ⋯ never does), and the app's
shortcuts act on the active row. `unread` adds the row's dot and an sr-only "Unread". `menu` holds
the row's items (Archive, Mark read / Mark unread, Unsnooze, Move to Inbox) after Snooze and Fewer
like this, and stays on a settled card; on a touch screen a long press on the card opens it.

### Compact

`compact` is the calm list row For you's list and Home's top items use: the verb's tile, the kind
(`kindLabel`, else the verb) and who asks with the `time`, the title, and one `why` line (who or what
asked, and why it waits on the reader). The row carries no buttons. A click on it, or Enter on the
Inbox's active row (`menuOpen`), opens a menu anchored to it: the card's options numbered 1–9 (a
link opens its page, Inspect its review), **Answer here…** (A) when the card has a body to fill, else
**Show details** (A), which opens the card in place under the row (**Less** folds it and returns
focus to the row), **Skip**, then the app's `menuActions` with
their keys (Open ↵, Mark done E) and **Snooze** (H). While the menu is open, a key runs its item.
Settled, a compact card is one line, what became of it and the title, with `onUndo`'s Undo while
the app still holds the answer back.

A settled card says where it was answered in the app's words: `app` "on the web", `phone` "in the
mobile app", `desktop` "in the desktop app", `slack` "in Slack", `email` "by email"; `system`
(nobody answered) says nothing.

## Accessibility

- The card is an article named by its title; withdrawn because access was lost, by its one line.
- The options are one group named "Options:" and the title.
- An urgent deadline and a fact's tone carry a named glyph, never colour alone.
- A failed answer is announced (`role="alert"`).
- In an Inbox, an unread card's name starts with "Unread:"; the active card is the current row
  (`aria-current`), and its title is the row's button when the Inbox opens rows.

| Key                                 | Action                                        |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| <kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>K</kbd>         | In an Inbox, move to the next or previous row |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd>                    | In an Inbox that opens rows, open the card    |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd>                      | Move through the options                      |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> | Choose an option                              |
