## Usage

```tsx
import { ForYouCard } from "@/components/ui/for-you-card";

<ForYouCard
  verb="approve"
  from={{
    name: "Expense checker",
    avatar: <PersonAvatar person={agent} size="2xs" />,
  }}
  title="Ravi's taxi claim, ₹4,200"
  facts={[{ label: "Over policy by", value: "₹1,200", tone: "warning" }]}
  deadline={{ label: "Due today", urgent: true }}
  ifNobodyAnswers="It goes to Finance on Friday."
  state={card.state}
  options={card.options}
  onOption={(optionId) => answer(card.id, optionId)}
  onSnooze={snooze}
  onSkip={skip}
/>;
```

The card is laid out for the 26rem Inbox panel first and the phone's For you page: one column,
facts and buttons wrap, nothing wider than the panel. It sits in `InboxList` as a row's content, so
the panel's keyboard and active row still apply (`selected`). Every card says what happens if nobody
answers.

## Examples

### Verbs and bodies

The verb shows as a small label with its icon: Answer, Approve, Review, Fix or Suggest. The body is
the source's own: a `question_form` source renders `AgentQuestionForm`, other `sourceBody` kinds
render through `renderBody`, and `children` holds an inline `AskCard`, a draft or a
`SuggestionCard` for a suggestion. "Fewer like this" is offered on suggestions only.

### Options

The options are the source's, in its order, with their kinds as the server names them. The
preferred one (`primary`) is drawn secondary: nothing on the card is a filled primary. `decline`,
`skip` and `neutral` are outline; `navigate` is a link; `inspect` opens the evidence and never
settles the card. Skip leaves For you without deciding, so it reads apart from Decline.

### States

`sending` keeps the pressed option's label with the bolt and holds everything else. `submit_failed`
says the answer didn't save and frees the options again. Settled, the card reads as its outcome
("Approved by Priya in Slack · 14:02"); a skip reads "Skipped", not as a decline. `stale` keeps
only its links to the new version. `withdrawn` with `access_lost` shows only "No longer available
to you": nothing of the source and no action.

## Accessibility

- The card is an article named by its title; withdrawn because access was lost, by its one line.
- The options are one group named "Options:" and the title.
- An urgent deadline and a fact's tone carry a named glyph, never colour alone.
- A failed answer is announced (`role="alert"`).

| Key                                 | Action                   |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd>                      | Move through the options |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> | Choose an option         |
