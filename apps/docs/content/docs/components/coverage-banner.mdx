## Usage

```tsx
import { CoverageBanner } from "@/components/ui/coverage-banner";

<CoverageBanner
  person={{ name: "Rohan", avatar: <PersonAvatar person={rohan} size="xs" /> }}
  text="Rohan covers your expense approvals until 30 Nov"
  action={{ label: "Change", onClick: edit }}
/>;
```

The words are yours: say who covers what and until when. A banner sits on a page with one action
("Change", "End"); `tone="warning"` marks a covered step that is urgent.
