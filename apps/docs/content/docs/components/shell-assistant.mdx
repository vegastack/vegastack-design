## Usage

```tsx
import {
  AssistantCard,
  AssistantCardBody,
  AssistantCardComposer,
  AssistantCardContext,
  AssistantCardHeader,
} from "@/components/ui/shell-assistant";

<main className="relative">
  …
  <AssistantCard id="assistant-card" open={open} onOpenChange={setOpen}>
    <AssistantCardHeader
      title="William"
      actions={
        <ToolGroupItem label="New chat" onClick={newChat}>
          <SquarePenIcon />
        </ToolGroupItem>
      }
      onClose={() => setOpen(false)}
      onOpenFull={openFullChat}
    />
    <AssistantCardContext onRemove={clearContext}>
      #3 Review Q3 pricing
    </AssistantCardContext>
    <AssistantCardBody>{messages}</AssistantCardBody>
    <AssistantCardComposer>{composer}</AssistantCardComposer>
  </AssistantCard>
</main>;
```

The card opens from the global bar's Ask button and the ⌘J shortcut; opening is yours (set
`open`), and the card sits in the page area — the positioned container you put it in.

The header's `actions` take the chat's own tools — New chat, Chats — as
[`ToolGroupItem`](/docs/components/tool-group)s, before Open full chat and Close.
Omit `onOpenFull` to hide it (no Agents module, no full chat).

The card grows out of the same corner, about 380 × 520 and never larger than its container less a
gutter. It renders nothing until it first opens, so the chat you put in it costs nothing until
asked for; after that it stays mounted while closed, so a draft survives. Both parts are
presentational: the host fetches and sends.

## Examples

### Three places

`place` puts the same card in one of three places (LAY-26). `dock` is a second card beside the
page, the split view: render the card as `Shell`'s `assistant` and the shell gives it 400px and the
page card's inset and corner; docked it is a region of the frame, not a dialog. `float` is the
compact card over the page area's corner. `popout` is a window of its own over the app: it opens
centred, drags by its header, resizes from its corner and stays inside the window.

Give the header `place` and `onPlaceChange` and it offers the other places: floating, "Dock beside
the page" and "Pop out"; docked, "Float as a card" and "Pop out"; popped out, "Dock beside the page"
and "Back into the app". `places` narrows the offer (a host with its own pop-out window, a phone).
Render one card in `Shell`'s `assistant` slot whatever its place — floating, it sits at the window's bottom-end corner over the page card; popped out, over everything — so nothing inside it remounts when it moves. Popped out, Alt and an arrow key move it from the keyboard (with Shift, further).

### Without a context

Remove the chip and the chat is about nothing in particular. Omit `onRemove` for a context the
person can't drop.

### On a phone

On a phone, put `AssistantCardHeader`, `AssistantCardContext`, `AssistantCardBody` and
`AssistantCardComposer` in a [`PeekSheet`](/docs/components/peek) (it rests at about half the
screen and drags up to nearly all of it) opened from the record's top bar.

## Accessibility

| Key            | Does                                                                 |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <kbd>⌘J</kbd>  | The host's shortcut opens the card; set `aria-keyshortcuts`.         |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Closes the card from anywhere inside it, unless a menu took it.      |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Moves through the header's tools, the context chip and the composer. |

- The card is a non-modal `dialog` labelled by the assistant's name. Opening it moves focus to its
  first text field; closing it returns focus to what opened it. While closed it is inert.
- Motion is the system's own and follows reduced motion.
