## Usage

```tsx
import { NavTree, NavTreeItem, NavTreeSection } from "@/components/ui/nav-tree";

<ShellColumn label="Home">
  <ShellColumnHeader title="Home" />
  <NavTree>
    <NavTreeSection label="Work">
      <NavTreeItem
        icon={<ListChecks />}
        label="Tasks"
        count={17}
        render={<Link href="/tasks" />}
        defaultOpen
      >
        <NavTreeItem
          label="My tasks"
          render={<Link href="/tasks/mine" />}
          active
        />
      </NavTreeItem>
    </NavTreeSection>
  </NavTree>
</ShellColumn>;
```

Every module's column lists its places with this one component: Home, Spaces, Library, Settings
and the rest. Items sit level with their section's title. A child is indented one step of the
spacing scale (12px), not by a chevron's width: a parent's fold control sits at the END of its
row, so a parent and its siblings keep their labels on one line. Children hang from a thin thread
line with a rounded elbow each, the way the Inbox threads a notification's events.

## Examples

### Parents and children

A parent with its own link (`href` or `render`) goes there on a click and folds from its chevron
or the arrow keys. A parent with no link folds on a click. `defaultOpen` sets where it starts;
`open` and `onOpenChange` control it. A parent that is itself `active` starts open; open the
ancestors of an active child with `defaultOpen`, so the column paints open on the server.

### Counts and row actions

`count` shows muted, tabular, at the row's end; 0 shows nothing. `action` (a ⋯ menu, a +) takes
the count's place while the row is hovered or holds focus, and always shows on touch, where
nothing hovers.

### Touch size

Rows are 32px, and 44px with `size="touch"`. Inside a shell narrower than 40rem, where the column
is a phone sheet, rows grow to 44px by themselves.

## Accessibility

- The current item carries `aria-current="page"`. A parent's fold control (and a parent with no
  link) carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` for its group.
- Every row is a link or a button, so Tab reaches each one.
- The section title is a heading that names its list.

| Key                              | Action                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| <kbd>↓</kbd> / <kbd>↑</kbd>      | Next or previous visible row                             |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> | First or last visible row                                |
| <kbd>→</kbd>                     | Open a folded parent; on an open parent, its first child |
| <kbd>←</kbd>                     | Fold an open parent; on a child, back to its parent      |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd>                 | Follow the row's link or press its button                |
