## Usage

```tsx
import {
  GlobalBar,
  GlobalBarCenter,
  GlobalBarHelp,
  GlobalBarHistory,
  GlobalBarLeading,
  GlobalBarSearch,
  GlobalBarTrailing,
} from "@/components/ui/global-bar";
import { ShellColumnToggle } from "@/components/ui/shell";

<Shell
  globalBar={
    <GlobalBar>
      <GlobalBarLeading>
        <ShellColumnToggle />
      </GlobalBarLeading>
      <GlobalBarCenter>
        {desktopHistory ? <GlobalBarHistory history={desktopHistory} /> : null}
        <RecentButton />
        <GlobalBarSearch
          label="Search or ask anything…"
          onClick={openSearch}
          ask={{ label: "Ask William", onClick: openAssistant }}
        />
      </GlobalBarCenter>
      <GlobalBarTrailing>
        <HelpMenu trigger={<GlobalBarHelp dot={unread ? "new updates" : undefined} />} … />
      </GlobalBarTrailing>
    </GlobalBar>
  }
  …
/>;
```

One bar runs the full width of the window above the rail, the column and the page, on every page.
It sits on the window's canvas (`--shell-canvas`) beside the rail, above the page's rounded card
(LAY-26). The centre slot stays centred on the window whatever the two sides hold. The workspace
and the account are not here: they are the rail's two ends (`RailTile`). In a browser, the leading
slot's first control sits on the rail's centre line, so the bar and the rail share one axis. The
column toggle hides itself on a screen with no column.

## Examples

### One box to search or ask

With `ask`, `GlobalBarSearch` is a 32px pill with an `Ask {name}` chip and its shortcut (⌘J) at its
end. The pill opens the app's search palette (open `CommandSearch` with `placement="bar"` so the
palette grows from the box); the chip opens the assistant. Give the chip the assistant's element in
`controls` and its open state in `expanded`. As the bar narrows the words fold to icons, the box
first.

### ? Help

`GlobalBarHelp` is the trailing slot's ghost pill: the help icon and "Help", with a brand-blue dot
while there is something new the person hasn't seen. Pass the words the dot means as `dot` ("new
updates"); they are read after the label. Use it as a `HelpMenu`'s trigger.

### The desktop app's title row

With `data-desktop-titlebar="macos"` or `"windows"` on `<html>`, the bar is the window's title row
(LAY-24): it drags the window and every control in it stays clickable. On macOS the leading slot
clears the traffic lights (`--shell-titlebar-mac-inset`); on Windows the trailing slot clears the
caption buttons (`--shell-titlebar-win-inset`). The desktop app writes both from its
`titleBarGeometry`, 0 in full screen. Pass `GlobalBarHistory` there for ‹ ›; a browser has its own.

### Phones

Under 40rem of shell the bar is hidden: a phone has its own one-row top bar and the bottom bar.

## Accessibility

- The bar is the app's `<header>`; F6 reaches it first, before the rail, column, top bar and page.
- The search box and its Ask chip name their shortcut with `aria-keyshortcuts` (⌘K and ⌘J on a
  Mac, Ctrl+K and Ctrl+J elsewhere); the printed key is hidden from screen readers. The chip
  carries `aria-controls` and `aria-expanded` for the assistant.
- Help's dot is decorative; its meaning is in the button's name ("Help (new updates)").
- Back and Forward are disabled when the window cannot go there.
