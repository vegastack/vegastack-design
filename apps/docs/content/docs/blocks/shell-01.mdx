## What it is

The shell reference: the module rail with counts and More, the Tasks column with views and a
spaces tree, the top bar in its fixed order, a task list, and a task opening in a peek over the
page. It is a **block**: you pull it once with `shadcn add`, and then you own it.

## Install

```npm
npx shadcn@latest add @vegastack/shell-01
```

<RegistryInstallCallout />

This installs the block's own files plus `shell`, `avatar`, `button` and `dropdown-menu`.

## Try it

- Click a task: it opens in a peek over the page's right side. ↑ ↓ walk the list, ⤢ opens the
  task's page, Esc or ✕ closes it, and the list keeps its place.
- Press ⌘\ to fold the column, or hover the Tasks rail item while it is folded to peek it.
- Narrow the window under 640: the rail becomes the bottom tab bar and the task opens in a bottom
  sheet that drags up to nearly the whole screen.

## Moving it into an app

`Shell` belongs in the root layout of the signed-in app, with `ShellStateScript` in `<head>`. Feed
`rail` and `column` from your routes, and open a record in a `Peek` inside `ShellMain`.
