A page has **one** vertical scroller and every scroller looks the same: a thin pill thumb in the
ink's own colour on a clear track, stronger while the pointer is over it. Two scrollbars side by
side, a page that scrolls under a board that also scrolls, or a scrollbar that appears for no
visible reason are bugs, not styles (BRD-SCR).

## The rules

1. **One scrollbar everywhere.** `base.css` sets
   `:where(*) { scrollbar-width: thin; scrollbar-color: var(--scrollbar-thumb) transparent }`, with
   `--scrollbar-thumb-hover` while the pointer is over the scroller. No component styles its own
   scrollbar. Two utilities are the only exceptions: `scrollbar-none` for a strip that shows edge
   fades or arrows instead, and `scrollbar-gutter-stable` when content must not shift as the bar
   appears.
2. **`ScrollArea` for the shell's regions.** The shell's own columns scroll in a `ScrollArea`, whose
   bar is an overlay: it takes no width, shows on hover and while scrolling, and its thumb uses the
   same `--scrollbar-thumb`. The secondary sidebar (`ShellColumn`) is one: its viewport is the
   `<nav data-slot="shell-column">` itself (`viewportRender`, `viewportProps`).
3. **One scroller per axis per region.** The shell's content area (`shell-content`) is a page's only
   vertical scroller. A view that scrolls inside it — a record layout, a `Board` with
   `height="fill"` — takes exactly the remaining height, so the page never scrolls as well. A fill
   board measures the real scroll area, less what the page lays out after it; `fillOffset`
   (default `0px`) is only extra room.
4. **A scroller is positioned.** Every `overflow-*-auto` or `overflow-*-scroll` element carries
   `relative` (or is itself `absolute`, `fixed` or `sticky`). Otherwise an absolute descendant — a
   screen-reader-only label — escapes to the next scroller up and gives it a second scrollbar; that
   was the task page's double scroll. `shell-content` and RecordLayout's main are positioned.
5. **Sideways scrollers always show something.** A horizontal strip shows an edge fade
   (`scroll-fade-x`) or arrows where it has more; never a hidden bar with nothing in its place.

| Need                                       | Use                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| A page's content                           | The shell's content area — nothing of your own           |
| A shell column, a bounded list or log      | `ScrollArea` (overlay bar)                               |
| Any other native scroller                  | `relative overflow-y-auto` — the global thin bar applies |
| A sideways strip of chips, tabs or lanes   | `relative overflow-x-auto scroll-fade-x scrollbar-none`  |
| A view that fills what is left of the page | `RecordLayout`, `Board height="fill"` — they measure it  |
| Content that must not shift as a bar shows | `scrollbar-gutter-stable`                                |

**Enforced by:** `base.css` (rule 1, one place, zero specificity); `ScrollArea` and `ShellColumn`
(rule 2); `shell-content`, `RecordLayout` and `Board height="fill"` (rule 3); design-lint
`scroller-positioned` on registry source (rule 4); `scroll-fade-x` (rule 5).
