🔧 **AppShellProgress**: a 2px indeterminate bar along the top of `AppShellContent`, shown at once while a page loads. **`motion-reveal`** (design-tokens utility): a loading placeholder that waits `--reveal-delay` (150ms) before it fades in — a fast load never flashes a skeleton, and with `AppShellProgress` a slow one is never a blank page. [docs](https://design.vegastack.com/docs/components/app-shell)
