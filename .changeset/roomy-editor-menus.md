🧩 **SuggestionMenu** and **TextEdit**: roomy slash and `@` menus, and phone sheets. `SuggestionMenu` gains `size` (`"default" | "lg"` — `lg` is 320px wide, up to `min(60vh, 440px)` tall, with the description as a second line under the label and a 28px icon box) and `presentation` (`"popover" | "auto"` — `auto` opens a bottom `Sheet` with its own search field on a coarse pointer or a window under 640px, driven by `query`, `onQueryChange`, `onClose` and `searchPlaceholder`); `useSuggestionMenuSheet(presentation)` says when it does, and an option's `descriptionShown: "active"` shows its description on the highlighted row only. `TextEdit` gains `menuSize` and `menuPresentation` for its slash and mention menus: on `lg` each built-in block says what it is ("Big section heading") and the full slash menu is grouped Basic · the host's items · Media · Advanced, with `TextEditSlashAction.group` naming a host item's heading ("Insert" by default). `TextEditMentions.sections` (`MentionSection[]` — `"people" | "agents" | "records"`) fixes the `@` menu's order: People, Agents (with the agent avatar), then each record kind, the nearest match first; a person's email shows only on the highlighted row unless two people share the name (`MentionOption.descriptionShown` overrides it). Every new prop is opt-in: without them the menus are unchanged. [docs](https://design.vegastack.com/docs/components/text-edit)
