🗑 **Shell clean-up — the parts the shell replaces, and items no app uses, are removed**, with no compatibility aliases (shell layout L22). Removed registry items, none of which any consuming app imports: `access-chip`, `animated-number`, `announcement-banner`, `country-select`, `region-select` and their `geo-data` lib, `field-type-link-step`, `formula-input`, `level-meter`, `rating-bars`, `timeline`, `upload-dialog` and the `use-optimistic-action` hook. `table` no longer ships the orphaned `table-scroll-region.tsx`. A consumer that still holds a copy of a removed item deletes it before `ds:pull`. The 11 deprecated props are gone: `RelativeTime.unitStyle`, `ActivityEvent.agent` and `CommentData.agent` (give the actor or author `kind: "agent"`), `PickerPanel.searchAction`, `SortableList.menuItems` (use `getItemActions`), a board column's `collapsed` (use `defaultCollapsed`), `FilterChip.anyLabel`, `RecordListMore.label`, `SpacePickerItem.secondary`, `LoadMore.label` and the `DataGridLoadMore` type (use `LoadMoreState`); so is `TranscriptSpeakers` (rename speakers in the turn headers). `PageHeader` loses `favorite` with `FavoriteStar` and `PageHeaderFavorite`. **`NotificationDot` moves to its own item, `notification-dot`** (import it from `@/components/ui/notification-dot`); `NotificationBell`, the `notification-bell` item and the `use-animation-replay` hook (`useAnimationReplay`, `useShakeOnInvalid`) are removed — toggle the `motion-shake` utility for an invalid shake. Block `notifications-01` sits in the `Shell`: its Inbox item is on the rail and the phone bar. **The parts the shell replaces are removed**: the `app-shell` item (`AppShell` and every `AppShell*` part, `pageGutterClasses`, `useAppShellAsideCookie`) — use `Shell`, `ShellMain`, `ShellPage`, `ShellSection`, `ShellBleed` and `ShellTopBar`; upstream's `sidebar` (now excluded, with `SidebarStateScript` and the `sidebar-menu-flyout` item) — use the shell's rail and column; `record-peek` and `use-record-peek-row` — a record opens as a shell panel tab; `RecordLayoutRail`, `RecordDetailsSheet`, `RecordLayout`'s `stack` and `useRecordLayoutWide` — a record's details open in the panel's Details tab, so `RecordLayout` is one column; `DocumentLayoutAside` and `DocumentLayout`'s `aside` — comments beyond the margin open in the panel's Comments tab; `SheetContent`'s `beside`; and `RAIL_COLLAPSED_QUERY` and `pageGutterOf` from `page-layout`. `HelpMenu` takes a required `trigger` (a `ShellRailItem`) and loses `label`. `vegastack-design doctor` checks `ShellPage` where it checked `AppShellPage`. Block `app-shell-01` is removed (start from `shell-01`); the other blocks sit in `ShellPage`, and `issue-detail-01` shows its properties under the title. **New: `vegastack-design owned-files --unreached`** lists the registry copies nothing in the project imports (followed through relative paths and `tsconfig.json` paths) and exits 1 when there are any, so a consumer's gate catches an unused copy its unused-code scan skips.
