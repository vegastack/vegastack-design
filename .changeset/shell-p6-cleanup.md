🗑 **Shell clean-up — the parts the shell replaces, and items no app uses, are removed**, with no compatibility aliases (shell layout L22). Removed registry items, none of which any consuming app imports: `access-chip`, `animated-number`, `announcement-banner`, `country-select`, `region-select` and their `geo-data` lib, `field-type-link-step`, `formula-input`, `level-meter`, `rating-bars`, `timeline`, `upload-dialog` and the `use-optimistic-action` hook. `table` no longer ships the orphaned `table-scroll-region.tsx`. A consumer that still holds a copy of a removed item deletes it before `ds:pull`. The 11 deprecated props are gone: `RelativeTime.unitStyle`, `ActivityEvent.agent` and `CommentData.agent` (give the actor or author `kind: "agent"`), `PickerPanel.searchAction`, `SortableList.menuItems` (use `getItemActions`), a board column's `collapsed` (use `defaultCollapsed`), `FilterChip.anyLabel`, `RecordListMore.label`, `SpacePickerItem.secondary`, `LoadMore.label` and the `DataGridLoadMore` type (use `LoadMoreState`); so is `TranscriptSpeakers` (rename speakers in the turn headers). `PageHeader` loses `favorite` with `FavoriteStar` and `PageHeaderFavorite`. **`NotificationDot` moves to its own item, `notification-dot`** (import it from `@/components/ui/notification-dot`); `NotificationBell`, the `notification-bell` item and the `use-animation-replay` hook (`useAnimationReplay`, `useShakeOnInvalid`) are removed — toggle the `motion-shake` utility for an invalid shake. Block `notifications-01` sits in the `Shell`: its Inbox item is on the rail and the phone bar.
