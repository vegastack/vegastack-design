🧩 Add the spaces and sharing parts Regent needs, restructure the Share block around an explicit Publish switch, and fix the audit's review findings.

- New `ResponsiveDialog` (a Dialog from 768px up, a bottom sheet with a handle and a safe-area footer below), `SpaceChip` and `SpacePicker` (My space first, a check on the current space, disabled rows that say why, `placement="title"` for `[General ▾] › New task`), `AccessChip` (one focusable chip for a record's reach that opens Share) and `ChoiceCard` / `ChoiceCardGroup`.
- `share-01` has Share and Publish tabs at the new `md` dialog size. Copy never publishes: the Share tab's footer is "Copy link", and only the "Publish to the web" switch calls `onCreatePublicLink`; "Stop publishing" confirms. Built-in rows are locked with their reason, a personal space reads "My space", `defaultTab` opens on Publish, and `onInvite` may resolve `false` to keep the chips.
- `Dialog` gains `size="md"` (512px). `PermissionMenu` gains `variant` (`ghost`, `chip`, `outline`), `locked` and `lockedReason`, and two-line descriptions. `SidebarGroupAction` gains `showOnHover`. `SpaceAvatar` gains the 16px `2xs` size for sidebar rows, `showLock`, a person-with-lock tile for a personal space and the `SpaceIcon` (Layers) export. `ColorPicker` gains `HUE_COLORS`, `variant="inline"` and a None swatch through `onClear`. `AppShellSection` with `measure="prose"` holds a start-aligned 720px column. `AvatarStack` gains `total` and `footer`, overlaps by 4px and draws initials from letters only. The comment composer puts attach beside Send on the right.
- The settings guidance and `settings-01` move to autosave, with confirmation for consequential changes and explicit Save only for drafts that must apply together. DataList's own empty state is the only empty state in a list, and its empty row keeps an inset.
- Board keyboard moves out of a `droppable: false` lane work again; date components render a clock-free absolute date without a request reference and re-render only when their label can change; `refresh={false}` freezes at mount; pickers mark the provider zone's today and presets take a `timeZone`; `MultiStepForm` never resolves a step index of -1; unsupported pastes show a toast; stale DataGrid focus requests expire; grid cards with their own controls are labelled groups; and stale focus-border and toggle-group docs are corrected.
