⚠️ **Inbox**: one row per record, tabs, and J / K triage. `InboxItem` gains `actors` (stacked 20px avatars, three then "+n", names read out), `events` (`InboxItemEvent[]` — `{ id?, actor?, text, time? }` — listed under the row behind a disclosure), `status` (`"pending" | "answered"`) with `answeredLabel` (the line that replaces Allow / Decline once answered), `value` (the row's identity for triage) and `menuLabel`; `count` now reads after the actors, on its own line under `meta`, through `countLabel` ("9 updates") and becomes the events disclosure, instead of a pill after the title. `InboxFilters` is now tabs — `tabs?: InboxTab[]`, For you · Updates · Archived by default (`INBOX_TABS`) — plus a separate Unread toggle (`unreadOnly`, `onUnreadOnlyChange`, `unreadCount`, `unreadLabel`); `value` / `onValueChange` take the tab's value. `Inbox` gains `activeId`, `onActiveChange` and `onOpen`: J / K (and ↓ / ↑) walk the rows with focus, the active row gets a start bar and `aria-current` and scrolls into view, and Enter or a plain click calls `onOpen` without navigating (a modified click still opens `href`). Breaking: `InboxFilters`' `extra` prop and its built-in All / Unread chips are gone (pass `tabs`, and the Unread toggle's props), and the `InboxFilter` and `InboxFilterOption` types are removed (use `string` and `InboxTab`). The `notifications-01` block follows. [docs](https://design.vegastack.com/docs/components/inbox)
