🧩 **TaskInline** (new) and **Bookmark** cards: one box per task, Make task in place, and a link shown as a card. `TaskInline` (`title`, `done`, `struck`, `status`, `assignee`, `due`, `overdue`, `checkbox`, `onToggle`, `onOpen`, `href`, label props) draws a task in running text — one round box, the title that opens it (a peek), the assignee's avatar, a due chip and ↗ on hover — and `MakeTaskPopover` (`open`, `onOpenChange`, `anchor`, `lines`, `onCreate`, `assignee`, `due`, `space` slots, labels) makes tasks from checklist lines in a popover anchored to the line instead of a modal. `TextEdit` and `MarkdownView` gain `onTaskOpen`: a linked checklist line draws as a `TaskInline` (its own box, round, is the task's; the chip's task glyph — the "second box" — is gone), with `mentionStatus` (and `InlineChipState` through the provider) answering `assignee`, `due` and `overdue`; Alt+Enter on the line opens it. `onMakeTask` now also receives `{ anchor }` (the first line's element), and an unlinked checklist line under the pointer shows **→ Task**. `TextEdit` gains `bookmarks`: **Bookmark** in the slash menu and **Link · Bookmark** in the paste popover, a card with the page's title, blurb, icon and site from the `InlineChipProvider`'s `linkPreview` (which gains optional `siteName`, `description` and `image`), stored as `[url](url "bookmark")` on its own line; `MarkdownView` draws it as `MarkdownBookmark` (also usable directly with `preview` or `fetchPreview`). New grammar helpers `BOOKMARK_TITLE`, `bookmarkMarkdown` and `bookmarkOfParagraph`. Every new behaviour is opt-in except that a bookmark line already in a document renders as a card. [docs](https://design.vegastack.com/docs/components/task-inline)
