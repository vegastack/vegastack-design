⚠️ The chat composer, rebuilt (agent chat ground up, Ph5). `ChatComposer` is two lines at rest with one hairline and only + and Send inside: + opens `addMenu` (ChatMenu items) or calls `onAttach`, and Send morphs in place through `state` — `idle`, `sending` (a braille `TextSpinner` inside the button), `running` (Stop ■, a crossfade and a small scale), `stopping` (Stop held). Files are `attachments` in one row above the text (56px image thumbnails, one-line chips with a middle ellipsis, a progress ring inside the icon, ⚠ with ↻ on failure); `steers` wait above the box (`↳ "…" · read after this step ×`); `ChatComposerNotice` sits on the box's top edge with a new `progress` tone (dots loader) and no `destructive` tone; `meta` takes the line under the box. Removed: `status` (use `state`; `streaming` is `running`), `hint`, `toolbarStart`, `toolbarEnd`, `hasAttachments`, `onRemoveLastAttachment` (Backspace calls `onRemoveAttachment` with the last id), `submitDisabledReason` and the 📎 and @ buttons (`handleRef.openMentionMenu()` opens the @ list). New: `ComposerMeta` (privacy note, Plan toggle, model, ring as quiet text), `ModelEffortMenu` (models with number shortcuts, an Effort submenu with only the model's levels, Recommended and a warning on Max, More models with unusable ones muted, ⌘⇧I and ⌘⇧E) and `ContextPanel` (share used, a segmented bar and a legend that adds up to the window, Summarize now). `ContextRing` is a still 10px ring with its share beside it — foreground, primary from 75%, the warning ink from 90% — hidden until measured, opening its panel on hover or focus after 150ms and on click; `size` and the string `label` are gone (`label` is a function of the percent), `parts` take an `id`, and `labels` are the panel's. `ChatMenuItem` gains `description` (a tooltip and the accessible description) and `ChatMenuSub` gains `note`, `open` and `onOpenChange`.
