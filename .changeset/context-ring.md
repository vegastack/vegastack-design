🧩 `ContextRing`: how full a chat's working memory is, as a small ring for the composer's toolbar. Blue under 75%, amber from 75%, red from 90%, a grey outline before anything is measured; pressed, the tokens used against the window, the parts that fill it and Summarize now, or the host's own surface with `onOpen`. `ChatComposer` gains `toolbarStart` (after 📎 and @) and `toolbarEnd` (just before Send or Stop) for the ring and composer chips.
