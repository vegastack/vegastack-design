🧩 **MeetingIcon** and the recording controls' words. `MeetingIcon` is one rule for a meeting's glyph wherever it shows, mixed lists included: a single person for one speaker, a group for two or more or while unknown, and the loading `Spinner` while it records, uploads or is processed (`meetingIconKind`, `MEETING_LIVE_STATUSES`). `RECORDING_ACTIONS` holds "Start recording", "Pause recording", "Resume recording" and "Stop recording" (Title Case `menuLabel` for macOS menus only), and `RecordingActionIcon` their glyphs: the record dot in the destructive ink for Start, then pause, play and stop.
