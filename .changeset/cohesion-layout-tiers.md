🔧 **Layout tiers**: one width vocabulary for every surface — `phone` below 40rem, `tablet` 40–64rem, `laptop` 64–90rem, `wide` from 90rem — as Tailwind container sizes and breakpoints (`@max-tablet/app-shell-content:`, `max-tablet:`) in `utilities.css` and as `LAYOUT_TIERS`, `layoutTierOf`, `layoutTierQuery`, `RAIL_COLLAPSED_QUERY` and `pageGutterOf` in `page-layout`. **AppShell**: the page gutter steps to 48px from 1920px; `AppShellSection measure="default"` holds a list at a default page's content width, centred, inside a `full` frame (width follows the content, not the route); `AppShellHeaderActions` moves a page's top-bar actions into one ⋮ menu by the bar's own width and drops labels to icons below 40rem; an open sidebar folds to its rail when the window narrows into the tablet span and reopens on the way out (`collapseRail`). **DocumentLayout**: the text stays centred when the comment margin shows (the start track mirrors the margin while there is room) its tiers start at the layout tiers (L from 64rem), and `measure="wide"` stops at 75rem (1200px). **Sidebar**: `SidebarStateScript` starts an icon rail collapsed on a tablet by default (LAY-13 amended; `collapsedWhen={null}` opts out), and a collapsed icon rail keeps a menu count in sight at the icon's corner — a pill up to two characters, a dot beyond (A11Y-17 widened). **Sheet**: below the tablet tier a side sheet drops its side border and pads the safe areas (OVL-22).
