🧩 **DocumentLayout aside**: `DocumentLayout aside="discussion" | "outline" | null` gives a page one non-modal rail after the text, and `DocumentLayoutAside` is the rail — a heading with its `count`, a × (`onClose`), and `tabs` (`{ value, label, count?, content }`: Open · Resolved · Page, Review while a review is open), the panel scrolling inside a rail as tall as the window. Any `aside`, even `null`, keeps the end track from the L tier, so opening the rail never moves or narrows the text (in `measure="wide"` the text and an open aside share wide's `min(100%, 1200px)` block, the text keeping its start edge). Below the L tier the discussion is a bottom `Sheet`, the whole screen below 640px; `kind="outline"` pins the contents from the XL tier, where the contents rail's hover ticks step aside. An open discussion takes the margin's place. Without `aside` nothing changes. Type rename: the native `aside` props type of `DocumentLayoutContents`/`DocumentLayoutMargin` is now `DocumentLayoutColumnProps` (`DocumentLayoutAsideProps` is the new part's props). `useDocumentLayoutTier` also accepts a ref to a part inside the layout. **CommentMargin** `quote={false}` hides each card's quote beside its highlight (default unchanged). **VersionList** `groupBy="day"` groups the rows under a heading per day. **VersionCompare** (new): a document's history at full size — the selected version or its changes (`DiffView`) beside the day-grouped list on resizable panels, with `actions` (Restore this version, Copy) pinned; on a phone the list first and the preview pushed in. **Dialog** `DialogContent size="fullscreen"`: the viewport less a 16px inset (none on a phone), header and footer pinned, body scrolling (OVL-16 widened).
