⚠ Agents are faces, not hexagons: a new `AgentFace` draws every agent as one of 18 preset characters in one of 9 palettes, and `PersonAvatar` draws agents with it.

- `AgentFace` (new, BRD-13): the Pebble from 40px and the Dot (a 5 × 5 dot face in the preset's own silhouette) below; nine states (`FaceState`) with a cue on the head's shoulder (thought bubbles while it thinks), badges on the Dot for the states that need a person, and CSS-only `motion-face-*` motion (MOT-15) that is still under reduced motion and without `motion`.
- `agent-face-spec` (new lib, generated by `tooling/agent-face-spec.mjs`): the presets and palettes; the generator fails the build on a silhouette that reads as a circle or a rounded square.
- Tokens: `face-<palette>-a|b|deep|mark|ink` for blue, violet, pink, orange, green, teal, ink, mist and glass, plus `face-glass-1..3`, `face-ring-a|b`, `face-shadow`, `face-grey-*`, `face-highlight` and `face-dot-ink`.
- `PersonAvatar`: `person.face`, `faceState` and `motion`; `agentFaceFor` and `AgentFaceStateProvider`. Removed: the agent hexagon, `mark`, `agentFallback`, `PersonMark`, `agentShapeStyle`, `agentShapeMask`, `agentShapeTarget` and `AgentShapeLayer`.
- `AgentStateRing` hands its state to the face (`faceStateOfAgentState`) instead of ringing it; `AgentPanelAgent`, `AgentEditBannerAgent`, `PlanAgent` and `DelegatePopoverAgent` take `face` instead of `image`, `mark` and `hue`.
