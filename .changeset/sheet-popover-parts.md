🧩 **EntryList** (new): a short, compact list of plain entries in a sheet, dialog or popover — `EntryList`, `Entry` (`row` · `stack`), `EntryMeta`, `EntryActions`. **Sheet**: `SheetSection`, `SheetSectionHeader`, `SheetSectionTitle`, `SheetSectionLabel`, `SheetSectionDescription`. **Popover**: `PopoverContent size` (`default` · `md` · `lg` · `fit`), `PopoverBody` (scrolls from 70% of the window), `PopoverAnchor` (an invisible trigger to point an opened-from-elsewhere popover), and `PopoverDescription` keeps line breaks and inks a `strong`. **Skeleton**: `SkeletonText` (`text` · `rows`). **Attachment**: `AttachmentSkeletons`, and `AttachmentDescription` sets numbers in tabular figures. **VersionList**: `headingHidden`. **Comments**: `CommentThreadSkeleton`. **DocumentLayout**: `DocumentLayoutDecisionBar` with its text and actions. **Badge**: a badge rendered as a button or link shows the pointer.
