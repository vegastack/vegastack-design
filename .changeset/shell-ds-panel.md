🧩 Shell Panel and Panel Tabs, the shell's one right-hand panel (OVL-27, API-45). `ShellPanel` fills the slot `Shell` gives it — beside the page, over it, or full screen and modal on a phone — with a 48px `ShellPanelHeader`, a start-edge separator that resizes it between 360 and 720px without a render per move, and the Escape rule (menus first, then an overlaying panel, never a docked one). `ShellPanelTabs` and `ShellPanelTab` are a manually activated tablist of real buttons: italic preview tabs, pinned icons, change dots, drag or ⌥⇧← → to reorder, a right-click or ⇧F10 menu (`ShellPanelTabMenu`), and `ShellPanelOverflow`, the ▾ with search and Recently closed. `ShellPanelView` keeps each tab's body alive under `<Activity>` behind `ShellPanelSkeleton`; `ShellPanelEmpty` is the empty panel. The new `panel-tabs` lib holds the rules as a pure reducer — tab references with canonical keys and a back-stack per tab, insert after the current tab, one preview tab, a soft cap of 10 with Undo that never closes a pinned or protected tab, auto-close, Recently closed — plus `serializePanelTabs` / `deserializePanelTabs` (`{ v: 1, … }`) and the `?panel=` codec; `usePanelTabs` binds it to React.
