🔧 **FilterBar** gains `FilterBarFacet` — a "Status: Open" facet on SearchableSelect, single or multiple, local or server-searched, pinnable and removable — and chips that edit in place: a filter's `editor` opens from its label and value (`FilterChipTrigger`), Escape returns focus to the chip, and `onEditorOpenChange` reports it. **DataList** rows become real links with `getRowHref` (and `rowLinkRender` for a router link): modifier and middle clicks work anywhere on the row, and `getRowLabel` names each selection checkbox.
