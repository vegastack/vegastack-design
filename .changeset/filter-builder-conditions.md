🔧 **FilterBuilder** edits flat condition rules: `allowGroups={false}`, a `prefix` that names the rule ("Required when"), `labels` for its own words, `conditionError` for a row's own message, and `summary="sentence"` for a read-only sentence. `describeFilter` and `formatRange` print a tree or a range as text, fields take `options` and a `unit`, and five value editors ship with it — `TextValueEditor`, `NumberValueEditor`, `NumberRangeEditor`, `OptionValueEditor`, `OptionsValueEditor` — picked by default from the operator's `valueShape`.
