🧩 **RecentStrip**: one row of the records a person opened last — up to `max` (6) compact cards (icon tile · title · one meta line) in a sideways strip with edge fades, ◂ ▸ buttons on a mouse and one Tab stop with ←/→/Home/End; a single line of chips under 28rem of its width (a phone). `collapsible` (with `open`/`defaultOpen`/`onOpenChange`), `loading` (same-shape skeleton), `emptyState` (otherwise an empty strip renders nothing), `linkRender` for the router's link and `headingRender` for the heading level. **SidebarMenuFlyout**: a sidebar row whose entries open in a menu beside it on hover or press — Spaces in the collapsed icon rail; `railOnly` keeps the expanded sidebar's inline group, and `badge`/`badgeLabel` work as on `SidebarMenuButton`. **AppShellHeader** `mobileActions`: the phone bar's own tools (Search, the Inbox with its count) before `actions`, below 768px only, with a 40px sidebar trigger beside them; **AppShellHeaderMobileAction** is one such 40px button with a count pill (`count`, `countMax` 99, `countLabel`) or a `dot`. **AppShellFold** `place="sidebar" | "menu"` folds the drawer's footer entries into the profile menu on a phone. **AppShellSidebar** `railSeparators` rules a hairline between groups in the collapsed rail. All additive: without the new props the shell renders as before. [docs](https://design.vegastack.com/docs/components/recent-strip)
