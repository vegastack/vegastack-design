🧩 **RecordPicker** and **RecordPickerDialog**: pick one or several records of the kinds the app passes in — a search over the app's loader (debounced, abortable, paged), a kind filter, recents while the box is empty, results grouped by kind, rows refused with a reason; `value` holds records so earlier picks survive any search, Enter never picks a row from a superseded search, and the dialog starts from `value` each time it opens. **DestinationPicker**: choose where something goes — spaces at the top of a folders-only `FolderTree`, folders loaded as they open, recent places, a folder search, and destinations refused with a reason that is shown and announced. **FieldTypePicker**: a searchable, grouped list of field types with their icons; a type with `next` opens a second step (`renderStep(type, { back, setTitle })`) under a back header. **FolderTree** picker mode: a node's `disabledReason` keeps the row visible and openable but `aria-disabled` with the reason as its description. **SearchableSelect** `renderTrigger`: your own trigger element (a `RecordChip`) over the same picker. **SelectContent** and **ComboboxContent** take `loading`, `error` and `onRetry` (API-38). **ItemGroup** `variant="flush"` for rows in a card (API-37). **Input** `credential={false}` keeps password managers off a name field (FRM-17). **TabsTrigger**, **Checkbox**, **RadioGroupItem** and **Switch** reach a 44px hit area on a coarse pointer (A11Y-24 widened). **PersonAvatar**: an agent with a photo keeps a purple bot badge.
