🗑 Shell Panel and Panel Tabs are removed (layout v2, D11): `ShellPanel` and its parts (`ShellPanelHeader`, `ShellPanelTabs`, `ShellPanelTab`, `ShellPanelTabMenu`, `ShellPanelOverflow` with Recently closed, `ShellPanelClose`, `ShellPanelBody`, `ShellPanelView`, `ShellPanelSkeleton`, `ShellPanelEmpty`, `usePanelTabs`) and the `panel-tabs` lib (the tab reducer, preview tabs, the cap, auto-close, Recently closed, the saved tabs and the `?panel=` codec), with their docs page, previews and tests. A record opens in `Peek` from a list, or on its page, where `RecordLayout`'s side panel holds the reference material. Record Aside and Document Layout now point at the record page's side panel. `AssistantPill` is removed too (the global bar's Ask opens the card now), and `AssistantCardHeader` loses `onMoveToPanel` and `moveToPanelLabel` (there is no panel tab to move a chat into). The shell's own panel goes with them: `Shell` loses `panel`, `panelOpen`, `panelWidth` and `pageMin` (and `data-panel`, `data-page-min`, `data-panel-mode`, `data-panel-anim`), `ShellPanelToggle` and `requestShellPanelOpen` are removed, `useShellLayout()` reports `tier` and `column` only, `ShellStateScript` reads the `vs_shell_col` cookie alone, and `@/lib/shell-layout` drops `SHELL_PAGE_MIN`, `ShellPageMin`, `ShellPanelMode`, `clampPanelWidth`, the panel sizes and cookie, and the panel branch of `resolveShellLayout`; `parseShellCookie(value)` takes the column cookie's value only. The `--shell-panel-width` tokens (`-min`, `-max`) are deleted. `shell-01` opens a task in a `Peek`.
