🗑 **Dead-code sweep — items and exports no app uses are removed**, with no compatibility aliases. Replaced in the apps: `suggestion-card` (by `for-you-card`), `request-card` (by `ask-card`), `memory-note` and `chat-details` (by the record side panel). Never pulled by an app: `data-list-skeleton` and `field-type-picker`. Shell Assistant is the frame only now that the chat draws its own header: `AssistantCardHeader`, `AssistantCardContext`, `AssistantCardBody` and `AssistantCardComposer` are removed with `tool-group`, their only consumer, and the card no longer labels itself (name it with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`). Also gone: `ShellTopBarLead`, `inlineChipClassName` and `VEGASTACK_MARKDOWN_VERSION`; helpers used only inside their own file stop being exported (`middleEllipsis`, `COLUMN_RESIZE_STEP`, `READ_ONLY_FIELD_TYPES`, `KPI_SPARK_MIN_POINTS`, `splitChipButtonClassName`, `recordPickerKey`, `SHELL_COOKIE_MAX_AGE`, `timeZoneAbbreviation`, `dayGroupKey`, `EMBED_PROVIDER_IDS`, `trackLexer`, `scrollParentOf`, `supportsViewTransitions`, `toolNameOf`, `humanize` and the `PEEK_*` sizes). A consumer that still holds a copy of a removed item deletes it before `ds:pull`.
